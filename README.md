PRIVACY POLICY
​Last updated: August 27, 2026
​This Privacy Policy applies to this mobile application (the "App"), which provides a mobile web browsing interface for the Internet Archive (archive.org) services while displaying third-party advertisements.
​1. ABOUT THE INTERNET ARCHIVE
The Internet Archive is a non-profit digital library offering free access to books, movies, software, music, websites, and other cultural artifacts. As a web interface for archive.org, this App allows users to access publicly available digital collections hosted on the Internet Archive's servers.
​2. DATA COLLECTED BY THE INTERNET ARCHIVE (WEB CONTENT)
When you interact with Internet Archive services through the App, data handling is managed in alignment with the Internet Archive’s primary privacy practices:
​Web Logs: Like most website operators, the Internet Archive collects non-personally identifying information of the sort that web browsers and servers typically make available, such as browser type, language preference, referring site, and the date and time of each visitor request.
​IP Addresses: The Internet Archive logs Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for system administration, security, and aggregate usage analysis.
​Account Information: If you choose to create an account or sign in to archive.org via the App, any credentials, uploaded media, or loan histories are stored directly by the Internet Archive. You can review and manage your account details at https://archive.org/account/.
​3. THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)
To support the development and maintenance of this App, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob automatically collects and processes specific device data off-device to deliver personalized and non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance:
​Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (AAID/GAID), Android ID, MAC address, or other unique software/hardware identifiers.
​Approximate Location: Inferred from your IP address to deliver regionally relevant advertisements.
​App Performance and Usage Data: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, diagnostic logs, and performance metrics.
​Data collected by Google AdMob is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS connection and shared with Google-approved ad network partners. To learn more about how Google handles this data, visit the Google Privacy & Terms Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
​4. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE
The App's WebView and the Internet Archive may place cookies or local storage objects on your device to maintain session states, save user preferences, and support site navigation. You can clear app data or cache at any time via your mobile device's system settings.
​5. DATA SECURITY & ENCRYPTION
We prioritize user data protection. All network traffic between the App, Google AdMob, and the Internet Archive is encrypted in transit using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
​6. THIRD-PARTY LINKS & CONTENT
The Internet Archive contains millions of external links, archived web pages (via the Wayback Machine), and third-party media items. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites accessed via archived web links inside the App.
​7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal or technical requirements. Updates will be posted directly to this page.
