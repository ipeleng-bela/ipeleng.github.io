PRIVACY POLICY
​Last updated: August 27, 2026
​This Privacy Policy describes how our mobile application (the "App") handles user data. The App functions as a web interface/wrapper for the publicly accessible Internet Archive (archive.org) services while displaying advertisements via third-party ad networks.
​1. DATA COLLECTION AND USAGE
We do not directly collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable user information on our own private servers. However, to provide core functionality and support monetized features, the app uses third-party services that may collect data used to identify your device.
​2. THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)
Our app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. To serve personalized and contextual ads, analyze ad performance, measure traffic, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob automatically collects and processes certain data off-device, including:
​Device and Other Identifiers: Advertising ID (AAID/GAID), Android ID, MAC address, or other unique hardware identifiers.
​Approximate Location Data: Derived from IP addresses to serve localized advertising.
​App Performance and Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, diagnostic reports, and crash logs.
